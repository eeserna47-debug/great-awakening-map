# Patent Suppression

The claim that inventions threatening to fossil-fuel or utility interests are bought up and shelved, or blocked outright via secrecy orders.

## Related

- Branch: [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]]
- See also: [[Deep State & Cabal Control]]

## Context

The US does have a real "Invention Secrecy Act" mechanism that can restrict publication of patents with national-security implications — a genuine, documented process — but its scope (mostly military/nuclear-adjacent) is much narrower than the sweeping free-energy suppression claimed here.
