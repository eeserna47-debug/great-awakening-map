# Free Energy & Suppressed Technology

Claims that clean, virtually unlimited energy technology has existed for over a century but has been deliberately suppressed by energy companies and the Cabal to preserve profits and control.

## Sub-topics

- [[Tesla & Zero-Point Energy]] — claims tied to Nikola Tesla's unfinished work and "zero-point" energy extraction
- [[Patent Suppression]] — claims that patent offices and national security orders bury inventions

## Related branches

Connects to [[Deep State & Cabal Control]] (financial motive for suppression) and [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]] (claimed ET origin of some suppressed tech).

## Context

Nikola Tesla was a real inventor whose unfinished projects (like Wardenclyffe Tower) are well documented, but "zero-point energy" as a usable power source violates the second law of thermodynamics as currently understood and has no working demonstrated implementation.
