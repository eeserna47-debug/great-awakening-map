# Ancient Aliens & Megalithic Sites

The claim that structures like the Pyramids of Giza, Puma Punku, and other megalithic sites required extraterrestrial technology or guidance to construct.

## Related

- Branch: [[Ancient Civilizations & Hidden History]]
- See also: [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]]

## Context

Archaeologists and engineers have reconstructed plausible human-built construction methods for these sites (ramps, sledges, copper/stone tooling, organized labor); "ancient astronaut" claims are considered pseudoarchaeology by the mainstream field and have been criticized for downplaying the achievements of the non-European cultures who built them.
