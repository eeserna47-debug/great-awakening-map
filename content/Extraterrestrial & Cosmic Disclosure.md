# Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure

Claims that governments have made contact with extraterrestrial civilizations and are suppressing "full disclosure" of this contact, secret space travel, and off-world alliances.

## Sub-topics

- [[Secret Space Programs]] — claims of hidden, advanced off-world military/space operations
- [[Galactic Federation & Contact]] — claims of benevolent ET alliances guiding humanity's awakening

## Related branches

Connects to [[Deep State & Cabal Control]] (the Cabal is claimed to be suppressing disclosure) and [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]] (ET tech is claimed to be the source of suppressed free energy).

## Context

Governments have declassified some UFO/UAP (Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena) footage and reports in recent years, which is real and documented — but this is far narrower than the map's claims of confirmed contact, secret space fleets, or galactic alliances, which remain unverified.
