# 5D Shift & The Great Solar Flash

Claims that a burst of cosmic/solar energy ("the Great Solar Flash") will trigger a shift of Earth and humanity from 3D to 5D consciousness, a recurring theme in the map's imagery.

## Related

- Branch: [[Consciousness, Ascension & The Event]]
- See also: [[Galactic Federation & Contact]]

## Context

"Dimensions" in this context is used in a spiritual/metaphysical sense unrelated to the mathematical or physics use of the term. There's no scientific mechanism by which a "solar flash" would alter human consciousness in the way described.
