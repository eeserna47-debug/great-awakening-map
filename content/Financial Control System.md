# Financial Control System

Claims that central banking and the dollar-based financial system are deliberate control mechanisms, and that a hidden "Quantum Financial System" (QFS) will replace it after disclosure, redistributing wealth globally.

## Related

- Branch: [[Deep State & Cabal Control]]
- See also: [[The Event & Full Disclosure]]

## Context

Central banking and monetary policy are real, well-documented systems; the "Quantum Financial System" is an unverified claim with no evidence of existing, popular in the same online communities as QAnon and "NESARA/GESARA" theories.
