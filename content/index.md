# The Great Awakening Map (Vault Edition)

This vault reconstructs the major branches of the *Great Awakening Map* — the 2018 poster created by artist Parinya Champ ("Art House") — as a linked knowledge graph instead of a static poster. Click any branch below, or open Graph View to explore connections.

> [!note] What this is
> The original map is a piece of conspiracy/New Age art, not a sourced reference document. This vault presents its claims **as claims** — what the map's community believes and asserts — alongside brief context. See [[About This Vault]] for scope and sourcing notes.

## Branches

- [[Deep State & Cabal Control]] — hidden global power structures, mass arrests, financial control
- [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]] — secret space programs, contact, galactic alliances
- [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]] — Tesla tech, zero-point energy, patent suppression
- [[Ancient Civilizations & Hidden History]] — Atlantis, ancient aliens, megalithic mysteries
- [[Consciousness, Ascension & The Event]] — 5D shift, the Great Solar Flash, full disclosure
- [[Health, Healing & Natural Sovereignty]] — suppressed cures, pineal gland, bioenergetics

## How to use this

Each branch note is a hub (MOC) linking to its sub-topics. Sub-topics link back to their branch and sideways to related topics in other branches, mirroring how the original poster visually clusters and threads its imagery together. Open Graph View to see the whole structure at once, the same way you'd look at the poster.
