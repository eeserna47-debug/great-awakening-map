# Ancient Civilizations & Hidden History

Claims that mainstream archaeology and history hide or misdate evidence of advanced prehistoric civilizations, often tied to ET or "lost" origins.

## Sub-topics

- [[Atlantis & Lemuria]] — claims of lost advanced continents/civilizations
- [[Ancient Aliens & Megalithic Sites]] — claims that megalithic structures required off-world help to build

## Related branches

Connects to [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]] (claimed ET involvement) and [[Consciousness, Ascension & The Event]] (claimed cyclical "ages" of human consciousness).

## Context

Mainstream archaeology does actively revise timelines as new evidence emerges (e.g. Göbekli Tepe pushed back known monumental construction dates) — but claims of lost high-tech civilizations like Atlantis or of alien-built megaliths lack material evidence and are not accepted by the field.
