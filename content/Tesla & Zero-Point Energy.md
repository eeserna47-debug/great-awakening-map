# Tesla & Zero-Point Energy

Claims center on Nikola Tesla's unfinished wireless-power research and the idea that "zero-point energy" (energy theoretically present in vacuum fluctuations) could be harnessed for unlimited free power, and that this was suppressed.

## Related

- Branch: [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]]
- See also: [[Patent Suppression]]

## Context

Zero-point energy is a real concept in quantum physics, but extracting usable net energy from it has no accepted theoretical basis or working demonstration. Tesla's funding for Wardenclyffe was cut for conventional financial reasons (his backer J.P. Morgan withdrew funding), not proven suppression.
