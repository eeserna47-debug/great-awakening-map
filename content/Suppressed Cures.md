# Suppressed Cures

The claim that cures for cancer and other major diseases already exist but are withheld by pharmaceutical companies to preserve treatment-based revenue.

## Related

- Branch: [[Health, Healing & Natural Sovereignty]]
- See also: [[Financial Control System]]

## Context

No credible evidence supports a suppressed universal cure for cancer (a broad category of diseases, not one condition). This claim is flagged by health authorities as actively harmful misinformation, since it can lead people to delay or refuse effective treatment. If this topic is personally relevant to you, consider talking to a licensed medical provider about your options.
