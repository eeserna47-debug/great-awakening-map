# Galactic Federation & Contact

The belief in a benevolent alliance of extraterrestrial civilizations ("Galactic Federation of Light" in some sources) that is guiding or waiting to assist humanity's spiritual awakening once conditions allow open contact.

## Related

- Branch: [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]]
- See also: [[Consciousness, Ascension & The Event]]

## Context

This concept originates largely in channeled New Age literature from the mid-20th century onward; it has no basis in astronomy or verified evidence and is treated as a spiritual belief within these communities rather than an empirical claim.
