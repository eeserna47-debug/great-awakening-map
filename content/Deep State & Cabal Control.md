# Deep State & Cabal Control

The map's largest cluster: the claim that an entrenched, unelected network — variously called "the Cabal," "the Deep State," or "globalists" — secretly controls governments, banking, and media, and that this control is now unraveling.

## Sub-topics

- [[Mass Arrests & The Storm]] — the belief that mass arrests of elites ("The Storm") are imminent or underway
- [[Secret Societies & Bloodlines]] — claims about hereditary elite networks and secret orders
- [[Financial Control System]] — central banking, the petrodollar, and claims of an alternate "quantum financial system"

## Related branches

Connects to [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]] (claims the Cabal has suppressed ET contact) and [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]] (claims the Cabal suppressed free energy for profit).

## Context

"Deep state" as a term of art originates in real political science (describing entrenched bureaucracies, notably in Cold War-era Turkey), but the sweeping global-cabal version used here is a conspiracy theory with no verified evidence, closely associated with the QAnon movement.
