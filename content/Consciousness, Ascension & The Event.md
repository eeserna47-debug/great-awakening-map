# Consciousness, Ascension & The Event

The spiritual core of the map: the belief that humanity is undergoing a collective shift into higher ("5D") consciousness, culminating in a singular transformative moment referred to as "The Event."

## Sub-topics

- [[5D Shift & The Great Solar Flash]] — claims of a coming energetic/cosmic shift in consciousness
- [[The Event & Full Disclosure]] — the anticipated culminating moment tying together mass arrests, ET contact, and financial reset

## Related branches

Connects to [[Deep State & Cabal Control]] (The Event is framed as including mass arrests) and [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]] (framed as including open contact).

## Context

This branch is explicitly spiritual/New Age rather than empirical — it draws on Theosophy, channeling traditions, and yoga/meditation culture. It isn't a falsifiable claim in the way "mass arrests by 2020" was, which is part of why it persists even as other predicted map events fail to occur.
