# Pineal Gland & Bioenergetics

Claims that the pineal gland (the "third eye") is a spiritual organ that can be "activated" through diet, meditation, or detox practices to unlock higher perception, paired with broader belief in a manipulable human energy body (chakras, auras).

## Related

- Branch: [[Health, Healing & Natural Sovereignty]]
- See also: [[5D Shift & The Great Solar Flash]]

## Context

The pineal gland is a real endocrine organ that produces melatonin and regulates circadian rhythm — well established in physiology. Claims of "activation" unlocking psychic perception are spiritual/metaphysical claims without scientific support, distinct from its documented biological function.
