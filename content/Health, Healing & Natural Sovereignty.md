# Health, Healing & Natural Sovereignty

Claims that the mainstream medical/pharmaceutical system suppresses natural cures in favor of profit, paired with practices believed to unlock spiritual/bodily potential.

## Sub-topics

- [[Suppressed Cures]] — claims that cures for major diseases exist but are hidden
- [[Pineal Gland & Bioenergetics]] — claims about the pineal gland as a spiritual organ, and energy-body practices

## Related branches

Connects to [[Deep State & Cabal Control]] (profit-motive framing) and [[Consciousness, Ascension & The Event]] (bodily "activation" tied to spiritual ascension).

## Context

Legitimate criticism exists of pharmaceutical industry incentives and access-to-medicine issues — but the specific claim of "hidden cures" for diseases like cancer is not supported by evidence and has caused real harm when it leads people to forgo effective treatment.
