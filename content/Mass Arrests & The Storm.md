# Mass Arrests & The Storm

A recurring claim in QAnon-adjacent content: that sealed indictments, military tribunals, or "white hats" within government are preparing mass arrests of corrupt elites, referred to as "The Storm."

## Related

- Branch: [[Deep State & Cabal Control]]
- See also: [[The Event & Full Disclosure]] (arrests are often framed as a precursor to "The Event")

## Context

No mass arrests of the kind described have occurred since the theory's origin in 2017. Predicted dates have repeatedly passed without the described events.
