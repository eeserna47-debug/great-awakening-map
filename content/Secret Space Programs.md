# Secret Space Programs

Claims of a hidden, decades-old space program — separate from public agencies like NASA — using reverse-engineered or ET-derived technology for off-world operations.

## Related

- Branch: [[Extraterrestrial & Cosmic Disclosure]]
- See also: [[Free Energy & Suppressed Technology]] (claimed power source for these programs)

## Context

No credible evidence supports the existence of a secret space program beyond publicly known classified military space assets (e.g. the X-37B), which are real but far more limited than the claims made here.
