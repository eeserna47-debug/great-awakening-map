# Atlantis & Lemuria

Claims of lost advanced civilizations — Atlantis (originating in Plato's writing) and Lemuria (a 19th-century theosophical invention) — that predate and technologically surpassed recorded history.

## Related

- Branch: [[Ancient Civilizations & Hidden History]]
- See also: [[Secret Societies & Bloodlines]] (some versions claim modern bloodlines descend from these civilizations)

## Context

Plato's Atlantis is widely regarded by classicists as an allegorical device in his dialogues, not a historical record. Lemuria was proposed in the 1800s to explain lemur distribution before plate tectonics was understood, then adopted by occultists; it has no geological basis.
