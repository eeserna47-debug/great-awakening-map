# Secret Societies & Bloodlines

Claims that hereditary "bloodline" families and secret orders (Freemasons, Illuminati, and similar) coordinate global control across generations, often blended with older 20th-century conspiracy literature.

## Related

- Branch: [[Deep State & Cabal Control]]
- See also: [[Ancient Civilizations & Hidden History]] (bloodline claims are often traced back to ancient or "off-world" origins)

## Context

Real historical secret societies (e.g. Freemasonry) exist as social/fraternal organizations; the specific claim of coordinated hereditary world control is unsubstantiated and overlaps with antisemitic conspiracy tropes in some of its source material, which readers should be aware of.
